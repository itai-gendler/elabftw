# Changelog entry

One recorded change to an experiment or resource, shown on its changelog: who did it, what changed and when. Changes to the main text point to the revisions instead of repeating the text.

## Information kept

- **Who** — the person who made the change
- **What changed** — the field or action, such as title, status, locked, steps or timestamped_at
- **New value** — the value after the change, or a description of the action
- **When** — the time of the change
